പഞ്ചാംഗം: തിഥി & യോഗം (Panchanga: Tithi & Yoga): Play With It

Enter a date, local time, and UTC offset. The widget computes the real sidereal Sun and Moon longitudes for that instant and derives the Tithi (their difference, divided into 12° steps) and Yoga (their sum, divided into 13°20′ steps) live -- reusing the same verified Meeus Sun/Moon toolkit built for the Vimshottari Dasha and Gulika pages.
